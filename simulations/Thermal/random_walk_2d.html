What to notice

Diffusion plus drift

Equal directional probabilities give no preferred direction and rrms = √N. A bias moves the centre of the cloud by Nμ while random fluctuations continue to broaden it.

rrms = √N + N(N − 1)|μ|2

Each jump is exactly one lattice spacing, a, along one of the four axes.
